01 What we do

Four kinds of dirt work
One crew for all of it

Most projects need more than one of these. Clearing a lot, taking down an old structure, digging the foundation and putting in the septic system can all happen with the same people and the same machines, with fewer handoffs and fewer delays.

  1. 01

    Excavation & Site Prep

    Getting a property ready for what comes next: digging footings and basements, cutting in pads, grading and trenching for utility lines.

    • Foundations
    • House & building pads
    • Grading
    • Trenching
  2. 02

    Septic System Installation

    Tank and drain-field installs for new homes and properties that aren't on city sewer. We'll walk you through what the job involves before anything gets dug.

    • New installs
    • Tanks
    • Drain fields
  3. 03

    Demolition

    Tearing down old houses, barns, sheds and outbuildings, then hauling off the debris so the site is clear and ready to reuse.

    • Houses
    • Barns & outbuildings
    • Debris removal
  4. 04

    Land Clearing

    Opening up overgrown or wooded ground (brush, trees and stumps) for a homesite, driveway, pasture or a usable yard.

    • Lots & homesites
    • Brush & trees
    • Overgrown acreage

Have a project that doesn't fit neatly in a box? Tell us about it. If it involves moving dirt, we'll let you know whether it's something we can take on.

02 How a job goes

Straight answers,
then straight to work

No runaround. You'll know what we're doing, roughly what it costs and when we can get to it before a machine ever comes off the trailer.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Call or send the form with where the property is and what you're trying to get done. Photos help if you have them.

  2. 2

    Look at the site

    We look at the ground, access and slope. Those decide how a job should be done in these hills.

  3. 3

    Get a clear estimate

    You get a plain-spoken price and a realistic idea of timing, so there are no surprises later.

  4. 4

    We do the work

    Equipment shows up, the job gets done as promised, and the site is left the way we said it would be.
