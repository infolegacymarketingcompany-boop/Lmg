Care for Every Season

One plan, all year. Here is what your lawn gets as the weather turns, so nothing gets missed.

  1. Spring

    Wake It Up

    • Cleanup and debris haul-off
    • Pre-emergent weed control
    • First mow and edging
    • Fresh mulch in the beds

  2. Summer

    Keep It Sharp

    • Weekly mowing and trimming
    • Irrigation checks
    • Shrub and hedge shaping
    • Spot weed treatment
  3. Now

    Fall

    Set It Up

    • Core aeration and overseeding
    • Leaf cleanup
    • Fall fertilizer
    • Final cut of the year

  4. Winter

    Rest and Plan

    • Tree and shrub pruning
    • Storm debris cleanup
    • Equipment tune-ups
    • Next season's plan
